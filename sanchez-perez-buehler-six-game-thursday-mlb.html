Thirteen Walks In Seventy Four Innings
Regional

Tigers at Mariners

Thursday, 4:10 PM ET | T-Mobile Park, Seattle, WA

Bryce Miller has walked 13 batters in 74 innings across 12 starts, the cleanest command number on tonight's board, and carries a 2.80 ERA with a 0.92 WHIP. Seattle's staff runs a 3.26 home ERA against a 4.32 mark on the road, one of the wider home-road splits in the sport, and tonight the Mariners are home.

Framber Valdez starts for Detroit at 4.41 ERA on the year, but his last two outings were his best of the summer, five innings and two earned against Kansas City and, on August 1, seven innings and two earned with six strikeouts against Oakland. Three starts before that he recorded one out and allowed six earned against the Cubs, so the sample is short but the direction is up.

Detroit already split the first two games of this series, an 8-0 win on Tuesday and a 4-2 loss Wednesday in which Bryan Woo threw seven scoreless innings. The Tigers are 25-32 on the road this season and specifically 17-23 against right-handed starters, a group Miller belongs to. This matchup, along with Detroit's own team total, gets a full breakdown on BetLegend's Thursday card.

Marquee Matchup
NBCSP

Nationals at Phillies

Thursday, 6:05 PM ET | Citizens Bank Park, Philadelphia, PA

Cristopher Sanchez is 14-4 with a 2.61 ERA, a 1.20 WHIP and 168 strikeouts against 33 walks in 144.2 innings, the best mark of any starter working tonight. His last start was six shutout innings with eleven strikeouts against Baltimore on August 1, a game in which he walked five men and still did not allow a run.

Washington is 56-60 and has lost eight of its last ten. The Nationals hung ten runs on this same Phillies pitching staff just last night, but that damage came almost entirely off the bullpen behind starter Andrew Painter, who worked four scoreless innings before departing. Sanchez is a different pitcher than whatever relief crew got hit, and that bullpen is now a night removed from unplanned extra innings.

Washington's own pitcher, Miles Mikolas, carries a 5.67 ERA and a 1.35 WHIP across ten starts, the weakest mark of any starter on tonight's board. Philadelphia is 61-54 and 30-28 at Citizens Bank Park. The Nationals half of this matchup, a team total under bet, gets a full breakdown on BetLegend's Thursday card.

Boston's Nine Of Ten
NESN

White Sox at Red Sox

Thursday, 7:10 PM ET | Fenway Park, Boston, MA

Boston has won nine of its last ten games and sends Ranger Suarez to the mound carrying a 3.15 ERA, a 1.16 WHIP and 105 strikeouts in 100 innings. He has not allowed more than three earned runs in any of his last four turns, including four scoreless innings against Toronto on July 26.

Chicago counters with Luis Castillo, who was traded from Seattle and carries a 5.06 ERA and a 1.39 WHIP built entirely with the Mariners this season, having allowed at least three earned runs in each of his last three outings there. Tonight is his first start for the White Sox. Chicago is 59-54 overall but 25-33 on the road, a losing record away from home walking into a club playing its best baseball of the summer.

Boston's own home record is a more modest 27-29 at Fenway Park, and the Red Sox are without outfielder-DH Roman Anthony, on the 60-day injured list with a finger tendon injury, and catcher Adley Rutschman, on the 10-day list with left wrist inflammation. Full breakdown, including the moneyline play, is on BetLegend's Thursday card.

Eight Straight At Home
BravesVision

Marlins at Braves

Thursday, 7:15 PM ET | Truist Park, Atlanta, GA | ATL -128, total 9

Atlanta is 69-45, has won eight of its last ten, and is 38-20 at Truist Park, one of the better home records in the league. Martin Perez has been the reason lately: a 3.24 ERA, a 1.17 WHIP and 75 strikeouts in 97.1 innings, with a seven-inning shutout start against Washington on August 1 that did not allow an earned run.

Miami sends Janson Junk, who is 5-6 with a 4.58 ERA and a 1.33 WHIP across 15 starts. His last outing was five innings of shutout ball against the Mets on July 31, sandwiched between rougher turns against Houston and San Diego. The Marlins are 58-57 overall with a stark split, 34-23 at loanDepot Park against 24-34 on the road.

The market has this at Atlanta -128 with a total of 9, a fair reflection of a home team that has been nearly unbeatable at Truist Park against a road club that has struggled to travel all year.

The Closest Price On The Board
Regional

Twins at Royals

Thursday, 7:40 PM ET | Kauffman Stadium, Kansas City, MO | KC -119, total 9

Kansas City is a modest -119 favorite behind Michael Wacha, who carries a 3.51 ERA, a 1.16 WHIP and 111 strikeouts in 138.1 innings across 22 starts. His last three outings have all gone six or seven innings with two earned runs or fewer, including seven innings and two earned against Detroit on July 25.

Minnesota counters with Bailey Ober at 4.45 ERA and a 1.23 WHIP, uneven across his last three turns: five and a third innings and two earned against the Cubs, four innings and four earned against Cleveland, then six innings and two earned against this same Kansas City club on July 30.

Both clubs are 5-5 over their last ten games. Kansas City is 48-67 overall but a very different team depending on the ballpark, 28-27 at home against 20-40 on the road, and Minnesota is the road side tonight.

A Rookie's Fourth Start
Regional

Padres at Diamondbacks

Thursday, 9:40 PM ET | Chase Field, Phoenix, AZ | ARI -112, total 9

Walker Buehler is 6-5 with a 5.18 ERA, a 1.45 WHIP and 44 walks in 104.1 innings, and he has not finished six innings in any of his last four starts. Arizona counters with rookie Kohl Drake, who is making just his fourth career start after ace Zac Gallen went on the 15-day injured list July 12 with right elbow inflammation.

Arizona has already taken two of the first three games in this series, winning 5-1 on August 3 and 10-4 on August 5, with San Diego's only win a 9-4 decision on August 4. Both clubs are playing well independent of each other, Arizona 6-4 and San Diego 7-3 over their last ten games.

Neither bullpen is full strength. Arizona is without reliever Justin Martinez, recovering from Tommy John surgery, and San Diego has already lost reliever Jason Adam to a shoulder strain. This series finale gets a complete breakdown, including both rotations' missing aces, on the featured game page.

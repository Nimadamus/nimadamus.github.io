Padres -151: Casey Mize At Petco Against A Struggling Eury Perez

San Diego has won three straight and 9 of its last 10, is 47 and 29 at Petco Park, and holds a wild card spot. Miami is 76 and 78 and 31 and 45 on the road.

Casey Mize has been a different pitcher at home this season: a 3.15 ERA and a 0.97 WHIP over 60 innings at Petco, against a 4.20 ERA on the road. On the other side, Eury Perez has had a rough September. His last four starts add up to 16.2 innings, 25 hits and 19 earned runs, and he didn't get through five innings in three of them. That includes 3 home runs allowed in Kansas City and 10 hits against the Mets. His season numbers, a 3.99 ERA with 155 strikeouts, say he's better than that stretch, and that's exactly why the price isn't bigger. I'll take the hot team at home against the pitcher who's been hit hardest lately.

The Argument Against This Card

I don't know who Boston is starting. The MLB feed had no Red Sox starter listed this morning. If they send a strong arm, the Rays price might look short, and Peralta's season ERA is still 4.67 even with the good run.

Bibee has been hit lately too. Two of his last four starts were 5 earned runs apiece, and he's lost 15 games. A bad Bibee day makes -177 a heavy price.

Eury Perez can still dominate. He has 155 strikeouts in 144.1 innings and a 3.45 ERA at home. One sharp outing and the Padres are in a coin flip game at -151.

Chandler walks people. 75 walks in 147.1 innings, and the Pirates have scored 738 runs, more than any other team in either of these unders. Walks and one big inning are how an 8 goes over.

The Blue Jays and Rangers combine for about 8.2 runs a game. The under 7.5 depends on the two starters pitching like their season lines, and both bullpens getting through the late innings clean.

Soccer - Monday, August 10

Plymouth Hosts The Devon Derby To Open A New League Cup

Four matches across four countries, and the biggest story is the oldest rivalry in Devon. Plymouth Argyle opens the 2026-27 League Cup at home against a freshly relegated Exeter City side, unbeaten across a six-game preseason. Around it: Santa Clara opens its Primeira Liga campaign without Nacional's top scorer from a season ago, and early-season fixtures in the Danish Superliga and Russian Premier League round out the board. Analysis only, no picks.

Marquee Matchup - League Cup First Round
Sky Sports

Exeter City at Plymouth Argyle

Monday, 3:00 PM ET | Home Park, Plymouth, England

Devon's two clubs open the 2026-27 League Cup against each other, the second meeting between them this year. Exeter won 2-0 at St James Park in the first, before Plymouth answered with a 2-2 draw at Home Park in April.

Plymouth finished seventh in League One last season, missing the playoffs by a handful of points, and manager Tom Cleverley's side carried that momentum through a flawless preseason, conceding just once across six friendlies. Exeter arrives in a harder spot, relegated to League Two on the final day of last season and now adjusting to life back in the fourth tier.

Both clubs treat the League Cup's early rounds as a genuine audition for fringe players, so expect rotated lineups on both sides. Even accounting for that, Plymouth's preseason defensive record and its higher league status make this the rivalry's form favorite entering kickoff.

Primeira Liga Round One
Sport TV

Nacional at Santa Clara

Monday, 3:15 PM ET | Estadio de Sao Miguel, Ponta Delgada, Portugal

Both clubs open the new Primeira Liga season looking to move past disappointing finishes: Santa Clara ended 13th a year ago, Nacional 14th. Santa Clara enters in the better shape, unbeaten through a five-game preseason with four wins, 12 goals scored and only two conceded, momentum that carried over from a late-season rescue under manager Petit, who collected 19 points from his first 14 games after arriving in February.

Nacional's preseason was choppier, one win in four completed fixtures, and the club now has to replace Jesus Ramirez, who scored 18 league goals last season before signing with newly promoted Turkish side Corum FK. Losing an 18-goal scorer in the same summer as a mixed preseason is a difficult combination for a road opener.

Santa Clara has also won the head-to-head recently, taking a 2-0 decision at this same venue the last time the two clubs met. The Azores have been a tough trip for Nacional, and nothing about this offseason suggests that changes on round one.

Two Winless Clubs, Three Games In
Match TV

Akhmat Grozny at Fakel Voronezh

Monday, 12:30 PM ET | Stadion Fakel, Voronezh, Russia

Three matches into the new Russian Premier League season, neither club has found a win. Akhmat Grozny sits 12th of 16 with two draws and a loss, two goals scored and three conceded. Fakel Voronezh is even further back at 15th, one draw and two losses, three goals scored against five allowed.

Fakel is the more porous of the two defensively, having already conceded five in three matches, and hosting a club that has at least avoided a loss in two of its three outings puts real early pressure on a Fakel side that needs a result at home to avoid an early gap to the pack.

Neither team has shown the finishing touch so far, combining for just five goals across six total matches this season. A tight, low-event match is the likeliest shape for two sides still searching for their first win of the campaign.

A Battle For Mid-Table
TV 2 Sport

Odense at Silkeborg

Monday, 1:00 PM ET | JYSK Park, Silkeborg, Denmark

Three games into the Danish Superliga season, Silkeborg sits seventh of twelve with a 1-1-1 record, three goals scored and four allowed. Odense is right behind in eighth, 1-0-2 with just one goal scored and two conceded, the lowest scoring output of the two clubs so far.

Silkeborg's extra point from a second draw gives it a slight cushion in a league where the top six splits off for a championship round later in the season, and every early point carries weight in that race. Odense's single goal across three matches is the number that stands out here. A club averaging a third of a goal a game needs its finishing to improve quickly to keep pace with a Silkeborg side that has scored in every match so far.

Home advantage sits with Silkeborg, which has the better goal difference and the more balanced record of the two clubs three weeks into the season.

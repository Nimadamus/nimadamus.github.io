Rush has filled in before, and his 2024 in Dallas (1,844 yards, 12 touchdowns, 5 interceptions) is the best version of the argument for him. But the most recent thing he did on a football field was complete 34 of 52 passes for Baltimore with no touchdowns and four picks, a 48.8 rating. This was not a dangerous offense even at full strength. Atlanta scored 20.76 points a game in 2025, 24th in the league, and went 8-9. Pittsburgh went 10-7 and 6-3 at Acrisure Stadium, and Aaron Rodgers is back under center after a 2025 of 3,322 yards, 24 touchdowns and just 7 interceptions.

The one thing to watch on the Pittsburgh side is corner Joey Porter Jr., questionable with a back injury. Jalen Ramsey was a full participant in practice. The forecast is 80 degrees with a 34 percent chance of rain.

Cardinals Under 18.5: No Conner, No Benson, And A Rookie Who May Not Play

Arizona opens under Mike LaFleur with Jacoby Brissett at quarterback, and the backfield is in pieces. James Conner is on injured reserve with a foot injury. Trey Benson is back on injured reserve with a knee. Rookie Jeremiyah Love, the name at the top of the running back depth chart, is questionable with an ankle. Guard Isaiah Adams is out.

The Chargers allowed 20.0 points a game last season, ninth fewest, and they are 9.5 point favorites with a total of 47.5. Do that math and the market has Arizona at about 19 points before anyone checks the injury report. This ticket sits a half point under the market's own number, on a team playing without James Conner and Trey Benson.

Brissett was genuinely good in 2025: 3,366 yards, 23 touchdowns, 8 interceptions and a 94.1 rating in 14 games. He was also sacked 43 times. Take away the run game and those sacks get easier to come by.

The Argument Against These Five

Arizona scored 19 or more in 13 of its 17 games last season. That is the strongest number against anything on this card, and it is why the Cardinals under is the smallest play at 1 unit. The case is this year's injury report, not last year's scoreboard.

ESPN's matchup predictor has Pittsburgh at 64.6 percent and Dallas at 59.1. Both are below the break even on those moneylines, 75.00 and 62.26. The bet on Pittsburgh is that a third string quarterback with a sore back, on the road, is worse than any general projection gives him credit for.

The Dallas defense was the worst in football. If the Giants get to 30, the moneyline is in trouble even when the team total over cashes. And week 1 is the week last season's numbers mean the least, for every team on this card.

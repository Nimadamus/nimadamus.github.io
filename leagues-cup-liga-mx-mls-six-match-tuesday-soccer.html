Soccer - Tuesday August 4

Eighteen Matches Against Three, Six Times Over

FC Juarez has scored one goal all season. Vancouver has conceded seventeen in seventeen matches. Those two sentences describe the same competition on the same night, because the Liga MX Apertura is three matchdays old while the MLS clubs hosting these six Leagues Cup fixtures are most of the way through a league campaign. Every match on this card is a team in week twenty against a team in week three, and the market has priced that gap very differently from venue to venue. Analysis only, no picks.

Marquee Fixture
Apple TV

Atlante at Vancouver

Tuesday, 10:30 PM ET | BC Place, Vancouver | VAN -390, draw +475, total 3.5

Vancouver leads the Western Conference with 34 points from 17 matches, a record of ten wins, four draws and three defeats, and the number that matters most is at the other end: 17 goals conceded, fewer than any club in MLS. A goal difference of plus 21 is matched only by Nashville in the East. This is the most complete team playing tonight by a considerable margin.

Atlante arrive tenth in the Apertura with four points from three matches, having scored five and conceded five. The recent league form line reads win, draw, loss, loss, win, which is the profile of a side still working out what it is. Three matchdays is not enough information to argue with, and it is also not enough time to build the kind of defensive structure that survives ninety minutes at BC Place.

The -390 price is the largest on the board and the 3.5 total is tied for the highest, a combination that says the market expects Vancouver to win comfortably rather than narrowly. The counterweight is form: the Whitecaps' last five league results read draw, draw, loss, win, loss, which is one win in five for a club sitting top of the table. Cup football against an unfamiliar opponent is exactly where a team in that patch can look ordinary for an hour.

Eleventh In The East
Apple TV

Atlas at Columbus Crew

Tuesday, 7:45 PM ET | ScottsMiracle-Gro Field, Columbus, OH | CLB -130, draw +275, total 2.5

Columbus have 20 points from 18 matches and sit eleventh in the Eastern Conference, with five wins, five draws and eight defeats and a goal difference of minus two. For a club with this stadium and this recent history that is a genuinely poor league season, and a cup competition is the obvious place to change the mood.

Recent results suggest the mood may already be turning. The Crew's last five league matches read draw, win, loss, win, win, which is three victories in five and the best short-term form of any MLS side on this card. Twenty six goals scored against 28 conceded across 18 matches is the arithmetic of a team that has been losing tight games rather than getting overrun.

Atlas come in ninth in the Apertura on six points from three, with four goals scored and four conceded, and a five-match league form line of loss, win, win, loss, loss. The 2.5 total and the -130 price on Columbus say the market sees a close, low-scoring evening. Both clubs have scored and conceded at almost identical rates in their respective competitions, and neither has a clear route to a comfortable margin.

Eighty Nine Goals
FS1

Pachuca at FC Cincinnati

Tuesday, 7:45 PM ET | TQL Stadium, Cincinnati, OH | CIN +100, draw +275, total 3.5

Cincinnati have scored 45 goals and conceded 44 in 18 matches. Eighty nine goals in eighteen matches is nearly five per game involving this team, and no other side in the Eastern Conference is close to that volume in either direction. The record is 7-5-6 for 26 points and sixth place, which is a strange way to arrive at a mid-table position.

That chaos is why this is the only match on the board priced at even money for the MLS side. The market is not saying Cincinnati are worse than Pachuca. It is saying nobody has any idea what the scoreline will be, and the 3.5 total, tied for the highest of the six, backs that up.

Pachuca sit thirteenth in the Apertura with three points from three matches, four goals scored and three conceded, and a five-match league form line of loss, loss, win, loss, win. A defence that has conceded three times in three outings is technically the tidiest record in this fixture, and it has faced a fraction of the volume Cincinnati generate every week. The home side's last five read win, loss, win, win, draw.

The Best Liga MX Record Here
Apple TV

Pumas UNAM at Charlotte

Tuesday, 8:00 PM ET | Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte, NC | CLT +115, draw +260, total 2.5

Pumas are fourth in the Apertura with six points from three matches, and their seven goals scored is the highest figure of any Liga MX club on this card. Two wins and a defeat with a goal difference of plus two makes them the only Mexican side here arriving with genuine momentum, and it explains why they are the shorter price on the road.

Charlotte are seventh in the East with 25 points from 18 matches, a record of 7-4-7 and a goal difference of plus two. The last five league results read loss, win, draw, win, win, so this is a club that has been quietly stacking points. Twenty nine goals scored against 27 conceded is the most balanced set of numbers on the entire board.

The 2.5 total with Charlotte at +115 and the draw at +260 is the tightest three-way market of the night. Two teams with identical plus-two goal differences in their own competitions, one of them fresher and one of them sharper, is a genuinely difficult fixture to separate, and the market has declined to try.

One Goal In Three
Apple TV

FC Juarez at Minnesota

Tuesday, 8:30 PM ET | Allianz Field, Saint Paul, MN | MIN -135, draw +285, total 2.5

FC Juarez are eighteenth and last in the Apertura, without a point from three matches, having scored one goal and conceded seven. Their five-match league form line is loss, loss, loss, win, loss. No club in either competition tonight is in worse shape.

Minnesota should be the obvious answer to that and are not. The Loons are tenth in the West with 24 points from 18, a record of 6-6-6 that is as balanced as it looks, and they have scored only 20 goals in 18 matches, fewer than any club playing tonight. Their last five read draw, draw, loss, draw, loss, which is no wins in five.

A 2.5 total on a fixture between the lowest-scoring MLS side here and a Liga MX team with one goal all season is not a bold call by the market, and the -135 price on Minnesota is modest for a home club facing the bottom of another league. Allianz Field is the sort of venue where a struggling visitor can be smothered, and it is also where a home side without a win in five can be held to a goalless hour.

Home Team, Away Ground
FS1

Real Salt Lake at Tigres

Tuesday, 10:00 PM ET | America First Field, Sandy, UT | RSL +135, draw +255, total 2.5

Tigres are listed as the home side for a match played at America First Field in Sandy, Utah, which is Real Salt Lake's ground. That designation is doing real work in the price: RSL are the visiting team on paper at +135 while playing in their own stadium, and Tigres are the nominal hosts at a venue they have no relationship with.

The Liga MX side arrive sixteenth in the Apertura with a single point from three matches, five goals scored and eight conceded for a goal difference of minus three. Their last five league results read loss, draw, loss, loss, loss, which is one point from a possible fifteen and the worst run of any club on this board.

Real Salt Lake are fifth in the Western Conference with 27 points from 17 matches, a record of 8-3-6, and they have scored 29 goals against 25 conceded. Their own recent form is uneven at draw, loss, loss, draw, win, but the gap between fifth in the West and sixteenth in the Apertura is the widest quality gap the market has priced closest to even. The 2.5 total is standard, the +135 on the team playing at home is not.

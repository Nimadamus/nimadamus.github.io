Fifteen Games, Misiorowski At 1.73 Against The Cubs, And Schlittler At 2.09 In Anaheim

Major League Baseball, Sunday September 2, 2026, fourteen games
Published September 2, 2026

Jacob Misiorowski takes a 1.73 ERA and a 0.75 WHIP into Wrigley Field against the lineup that has scored more runs than anyone in baseball, and Cam Schlittler takes a 2.09 and 201 strikeouts into Anaheim against a lineup that has lost eight of ten.

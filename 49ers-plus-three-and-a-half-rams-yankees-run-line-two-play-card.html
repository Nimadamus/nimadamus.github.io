Why 3.5 Is The Number That Matters

Three is the most common margin in professional football and nothing else is close. Every half point that carries a spread across 3 is worth more than any other half point on the board, which is why +3.5 at -110 is a different bet from +3 at -110 even though the prices look identical.

What the extra half point buys is the outcome where the Rams win by exactly a field goal. On +3 that result is a push. Here it is a winner.

Between two clubs that finished with the same record and split their series by scores of 26 to 23 and 42 to 26, a three point Rams win is not some tail case. It is the single most likely margin on the board, and this ticket already owns it.

The Margin Problem With Laying -230

This is where honesty is more useful than enthusiasm, because the margin history does not support the price on its own.

New York has won by 2 or more runs in 66 of 145 games this season. That is 45.5 percent. At home it is 30 of 70, which is 42.9 percent. In the 17 games Fried has started, the Yankees have won by 2 or more exactly 7 times.

A price of -230 needs 69.70 percent. Nothing in that paragraph gets anywhere near it, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

What pushes the number up tonight is the other side of the ledger. Colorado has lost by 2 or more in 34 of its 74 road games, 45.9 percent, and lost by exactly one run 15 times. Those 15 are the push column here, not the loss column, which is the entire reason this is a -1 rather than a -1.5. Take the pushes out of the denominator and a run line that gives the money back at one run reads considerably better than the raw 45.9.

That is the honest version of the case. It is a matchup bet on the widest starting pitcher gap of the night, sized at 1.5 units rather than 3 because the price is doing damage.

The Argument Against Both

The Rams scored 81 more points than the 49ers last season and won the most recent meeting by 16 at San Francisco. Home field, a full offseason, and the better differential all point the same way.

Week 1 numbers are seven months old. Every football figure on this page is from the 2025 season, because the 2026 season starts tonight for these two. Rosters moved. Nobody has seen these teams play a snap that counts since January.

The Yankees price is genuinely expensive. At -230 a one run win returns nothing and a loss costs 3.45 units. That is the worst risk shape on the card and it is why the unit number is small.

Colorado just faced this pitching staff last night. Second and third looks at the same arms inside a series are worth something, even to a bad offense.

Bottom Line

Two teams that both went 12-5 and split their series, and the one getting 3.5 points is the one that already won in that building. Take the points and own the field goal margin.

In the Bronx, a 2.73 ERA with a 0.99 WHIP against a 5.91 with a 1.58, and a road offense hitting .237 walking into it. The price asks for a lot and the season long margin numbers do not deliver it on their own. The matchup does, and the ticket is sized accordingly.

Soccer - Friday, August 7

Three Liga MX Clubs Lost To MLS On Tuesday, And All Three Are Back Friday

Atlas, Pachuca and Pumas UNAM were beaten by a combined nine goals to two in the Leagues Cup opening round on Tuesday. Friday hands each of them a different MLS host and a second chance at the same argument. Behind those five ties, Gotham welcome San Diego Wave to Red Bull Arena in the strongest league fixture on the board, and Club Brugge open at home in Belgium. Analysis only, no picks.

Marquee Match
NWSL

San Diego Wave at Gotham FC

Friday, 8:00 PM ET | Red Bull Arena, Harrison, NJ

Gotham are 11-4-3 across eighteen matches, San Diego are 10-2-6 across the same number, and those are the two best records among the four NWSL clubs playing Friday night. Thirty-seven points against thirty-two. This is the fixture the schedule owed everyone.

The difference between them lives at the back. Gotham have conceded twelve goals in eighteen matches, fewer than any of the four NWSL clubs in action Friday, while scoring twenty-three. San Diego have scored more, twenty-four, but have shipped nineteen. One team wins by suffocating a match, the other by trading in it, and Red Bull Arena will decide which style gets to dictate.

Gotham are the home side and are priced at +110, with San Diego on the other end and the total set at 2.5. A home favourite being the plus-money side is unusual, and it reflects a Wave team that has taken points in four of its last five. Gotham's recent run reads WWDWW. Neither club has been losing lately, which is exactly why the market has stayed close.

Three Goals Apiece On Tuesday
Leagues Cup / Apple TV

Pumas UNAM at FC Cincinnati

Friday, 8:00 PM ET | TQL Stadium, Cincinnati, OH

Both clubs were involved in a 3-1 on Tuesday, on opposite ends of it. Cincinnati beat Pachuca 3-1 at this same stadium. Pumas were beaten 3-0 at Charlotte, the heaviest loss any Liga MX side took in the opening round. Now the winners host the side that was taken apart.

Cincinnati's recent run reads WWLWW, four wins in five, and TQL Stadium has been the engine of it. They are priced at -110 with the total at 3.5, which is a book acknowledging both an in-form home side and a Pumas team capable of forcing a shootout if it defends better than it did on Tuesday.

Pumas arrive on a run of LWWLL. Three goals conceded without reply against Charlotte is the number that will define their preparation, because Leagues Cup phase-one standings are settled on results in a small handful of matches and there is no time to recover from two performances like that. Whatever they change, they have to change it in seventy-two hours.

The 3-0 Hosts
Leagues Cup / Apple TV

Atlas at Charlotte FC

Friday, 7:30 PM ET | Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte, NC

Charlotte opened the tournament with a 3-0 win over Pumas UNAM at Bank of America Stadium, the most emphatic result of the opening round. Atlas opened by losing 3-1 at Columbus. The two clubs meet with their tournament positions already pointing in opposite directions.

Charlotte's form line reads WLWDW across their last five in all competitions, a mixed sequence that the Pumas result sits at the front of. Playing a Leagues Cup tie inside an NFL stadium gives them a pitch and an atmosphere that few Liga MX visitors get to rehearse for, and it showed on Tuesday.

Atlas come in on LLWWL. Their problem on Tuesday was not effort, it was that Columbus scored three, and Atlas now face a side that just kept a clean sheet against comparable opposition. No moneyline or total has been posted for this tie, which is common enough for the less-watched fixtures in a competition this compressed.

Two Clubs, One Result Each
Leagues Cup / Apple TV

Pachuca at Columbus Crew

Friday, 7:30 PM ET | ScottsMiracle-Gro Field, Columbus, OH

Columbus beat Atlas 3-1 on Tuesday. Pachuca lost 3-1 at Cincinnati the same night. Friday puts the two of them together, which means the Crew get a Liga MX side that has already been beaten by exactly the scoreline the Crew already produced.

Columbus arrive on a five-match run of WDWLW. They have been the more coherent of the two teams for weeks, and ScottsMiracle-Gro Field is a genuinely difficult trip in this competition. Three goals in the opening tie is the kind of statement that shifts how the rest of a group treats you.

Pachuca's form line reads LLLWL, one win in five, and that win did not come in this tournament. A club with Pachuca's history struggling this badly for results is the sort of thing that usually corrects, but it has to correct against an opponent that has just scored three. No market numbers have been posted for this tie.

A Home Game For The Away Side
Leagues Cup / Apple TV

Minnesota United vs Tigres UANL

Friday, 9:00 PM ET | Allianz Field, Saint Paul, MN

The strangest fixture on the board. Tigres UANL are listed as the home side, and the match is staged at Allianz Field, which is Minnesota United's own stadium. Minnesota are the designated visitors in Saint Paul, and the market has followed the designation rather than the geography: Tigres are +125 with the total at 2.5.

Tigres opened their tournament with a 1-1 draw against Real Salt Lake and then won the shootout 6-5, eleven penalties before anyone missed. Their form line across the last five in all competitions reads WLDLL, so the shootout was as much survival as it was momentum.

Minnesota lost their opener 2-1 to FC Juarez, at this same ground, which is the detail that makes this assignment awkward. They have now been beaten once at Allianz Field in this competition and return to it as the away team. Their last five read LDDLD, five matches without a win, and the total of 2.5 reflects a side that has not been scoring.

The Only Big Price
Leagues Cup / Apple TV

FC Juarez at Vancouver Whitecaps

Friday, 10:30 PM ET | BC Place, Vancouver, BC

Vancouver are -275, the shortest price posted anywhere on Friday's soccer board, with the total at 3.5. That number ignores what actually happened on Tuesday, when the Whitecaps lost 1-0 to Atlante at this same BC Place, and it ignores that Juarez won their opener 2-1 at Minnesota.

The market is pricing the season rather than the week. Vancouver's five-match form reads LDDLW and Juarez's reads WLLLW, two sequences that look almost identical, and yet the gap in price is enormous. Books setting Leagues Cup lines lean heavily on club strength and roster value, and this is what that looks like when recent results disagree.

The case for Vancouver is the venue and the fixture's late kickoff, which puts Juarez on the field at 8:30 in the evening in their own time zone after a long trip north. The case against is that they were shut out at home three days ago by a side that most models would rate below Juarez. This is the tie on the board where the number and the evidence disagree most.

Fourteen Points Apart
NWSL

Racing Louisville at Orlando Pride

Friday, 7:00 PM ET | Inter&Co Stadium, Orlando, FL

Orlando are 7-2-9 and Racing Louisville are 4-2-11, twenty-three points against fourteen, the widest gap between two clubs meeting on Friday's board. Orlando have scored twenty-two and conceded twenty-six. Louisville have scored twenty-one and conceded thirty-four, the most of the four NWSL clubs playing Friday.

Orlando's five-match run reads LWLLW, which is not the form of a side pulling away from anyone, and their minus-four goal difference explains a lot about why a team with seven wins sits on twenty-three points. They are, however, at home, where the Pride have been the more convincing version of themselves.

Louisville's counter is that they have already beaten Orlando 3-2 this season. That result is the outlier in a season otherwise defined by conceding, and it is the single piece of evidence Louisville can point to that this fixture is winnable. Their form line reads WLWDL. No market numbers have been posted for this match.

Belgium's Early Kickoff
Belgian Pro League

KV Kortrijk at Club Brugge

Friday, 2:45 PM ET | Jan Breydel Stadium, Bruges, Belgium

The first kickoff of the day, hours before anything in North America. Club Brugge have won their opening match of the Belgian Pro League season and carry a five-match form line of WWDWW across all competitions, the joint-best sequence of any club playing Friday alongside Gotham.

Kortrijk lost their opener and come in on LDWLW. The Jan Breydel Stadium is one of the least forgiving away trips in Belgian football, and the visitors are arriving there in the second week of a season with a result already to make up.

Because it is so early in the Belgian campaign, there is very little to read from the table. What there is points one direction: Brugge have started the way a club expected to compete for the title starts, and Kortrijk have not. No moneyline or total has been posted for this match.

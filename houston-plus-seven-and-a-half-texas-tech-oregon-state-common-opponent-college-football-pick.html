One Common Opponent, Two Very Different Nights

Each of these teams has played one FCS opponent and Oregon State. The FCS games tell you nothing. Houston beat Southern 77 to 6 and Texas Tech beat Abilene Christian 33 to 10, and I'm not reading anything into either. The Oregon State games are where the comparison lives.

What Houston Has Looked Like

Conner Weigman is 29 of 42 for 425 yards through two games, with 4 touchdowns and 1 interception. Against Oregon State he threw for 305 at better than 10 yards an attempt, and Amare Thomas caught 8 balls for 133 of those yards. That's a real passing game against a real opponent.

The run game is the bigger story. Makhi Hughes went for 98 yards on 17 carries against the Beavers and Re'Shaun Sanford II added 75 on 11, and Houston ran for 228 as a team. A road underdog that can run the ball shortens the game, and a shorter game is a friend to anybody holding 7.5 points.

Texas Tech's offense has been steady rather than explosive. Will Hammond is 46 of 60 for 487 yards with 2 touchdowns and 1 interception, and J'Koby Williams has 187 rushing yards on 33 carries. That's a good offense. Through two games it hasn't been a better one than Houston's.

The Number Has Already Moved Five Points

DraftKings opened this game at Texas Tech -12.5, according to the odds feed on ESPN's game page. It sits at 7.5 this afternoon. That's five points of movement toward Houston, which means the market has read the Oregon State results the same way I have.

That cuts both ways, and I'll be honest about it. The best of the number is gone. Anybody who took Houston +12.5 at the open has a better ticket than this one. But 7.5 still sits on the right side of the touchdown, and the case above doesn't need the extra five points to work.

The Argument Against This Play

Houston hasn't left home yet. Both Cougar wins came in Houston, and Lubbock on a Friday night in a ranked conference game is a different assignment. Texas Tech has already won a road game this year, and Houston hasn't played one.

Houston's secondary leaked too. Oregon State threw for 376 yards against the Cougars. The Beavers got nowhere on the ground and beat the Cougars through the air instead, and Hammond completing 77 percent of his passes is the obvious way this goes wrong.

Houston turned it over twice against Oregon State, one of them a Weigman interception. Giving Texas Tech short fields at home is how a 7.5 point spread turns into a 17 point loss.

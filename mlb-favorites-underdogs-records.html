Methodology

Final scores and closing moneylines come from the Bet Legend game database, with every game checked against ESPN's official schedule and final score. Units risk 1 unit per game at the closing price. These are market results, not BetLegend picks.

FAQ

How often do MLB favorites win?

Since 2016, moneyline favorites won 58.1% of regular season games (14402-10397). The bigger the favorite, the more often it wins, as the price table shows.

Is it profitable to bet every MLB underdog?

Not blindly: one unit on every underdog at the closing price returned -626.90u (-2.5%) since 2016; every favorite returned -904.18u (-3.6%).

How do home underdogs do in MLB?

3892-5086 since 2016 for -201.47u at closing prices.

What does implied win % mean?

The win rate the closing price implies (with the bookmaker's margin included). When the actual win % is above it, that group of bets made money.

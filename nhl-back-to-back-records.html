Methodology

Final scores and closing lines for every regular season game since 2016-17 come from the Bet Legend game database, each checked against ESPN's official final score. Moneyline units risk 1 unit at the closing price. These are market results, not BetLegend picks.

FAQ

How do NHL teams do on the second night of a back to back?

Since 2016-17: 1650-2042 (44.7%) and -276.07u for a 1 unit moneyline bettor at closing prices, over 3692 games.

Should you bet against tired road teams?

When the home team was rested and the road team was on a back to back, home teams went 1090-745 for +24.31u on the moneyline.

Do back to backs affect NHL totals?

Games where a team played the second night went 1844-1764-84 against the closing total from that team's side.

What counts as a back to back?

A game played the day after the team's previous regular season game. Results include overtime and shootouts.

Soccer - Tuesday, August 18

Seven Champions League Places Are Left, And Three Of Them Start Tonight

Seven places in the Champions League league phase are still unclaimed, and the playoff round settles all of them across two legs. Three first legs are played tonight, all at 3:00 PM ET: Fenerbahce host Lyon in the League Path, Dinamo Zagreb host Viking in the Champions Path, and Levski Sofia host AEK Athens in a tie where the market makes the draw the shortest price of the three outcomes. Second legs follow on August 25 and 26. Analysis only, no picks.

The Road Side Is The Favourite

Fenerbahce vs Lyon

Tuesday, 3:00 PM ET | Istanbul, Turkey | Champions League playoff round, first leg

This is the tie of the round and the market knows it. DraftKings prices Lyon at -125 to win in Istanbul, with Fenerbahce at +400 and the draw at +220. A visiting side being the outright favourite in the first leg of a two-legged European qualifier is unusual on its own. It is more unusual when the home ground is one of the loudest in Europe.

The UEFA club coefficients explain the shape of it. Lyon enter at 65.750 and Fenerbahce at 57.750, the two highest ratings anywhere in this round, and both clubs arrive through the League Path, the route reserved for teams that did not win their domestic league. Neither of these is a qualifier making up the numbers.

Both came through the third qualifying round without much trouble. Fenerbahce eliminated Sturm Graz 3-0 on aggregate and closed it out with a 1-0 win in Austria. Lyon put Sparta Praha away 4-2 on aggregate after a 3-0 home leg. The second leg is on August 25 or 26, which means whatever happens tonight, nothing is finished.

Seven Goals In The Last Round

Dinamo Zagreb vs Viking

Tuesday, 3:00 PM ET | Zagreb, Croatia | Champions League playoff round, first leg

Dinamo Zagreb did not need two legs to settle the previous round. They put Kauno Zalgiris away 7-1 on aggregate, taking the away leg 2-1 to complete it, and they arrive with a UEFA coefficient of 46.5 against 8.247 for Viking. That is the widest ranking gap of any tie played tonight.

The pricing does not reflect the gap. Dinamo are only -120 to win the first leg, Viking are +450 and the draw sits at +190. In a Champions Path fixture where the home side is five and a half times better rated by coefficient, a -120 price is the market pricing in the format rather than the teams: first legs are cagey, and Dinamo have no reason to chase a three-goal cushion in August.

Viking come through the Norwegian route into the Champions Path, the bracket set aside for domestic champions from smaller associations. Their reward for surviving it is a trip to Zagreb, then a home leg on August 25 or 26 with the tie still alive if they keep it close.

The Draw Is The Shortest Price On The Board

Levski Sofia vs AEK Athens

Tuesday, 3:00 PM ET | Sofia, Bulgaria | Champions League playoff round, first leg

Look at the three prices in order: draw +115, AEK Athens +180, Levski Sofia +310. The draw is favoured over both teams, and the home side is the longest of the three. Across all three ties tonight, this is the only market where the tie ending level is the most likely single outcome.

Levski earned the trip. They knocked out Kairat Almaty 2-0 on aggregate, and the decisive leg was a 1-0 win away in Almaty. That is the sort of result that gets ignored because of the name on the shirt: their UEFA coefficient is 7.0, the lowest of any club playing tonight.

AEK Athens carry a coefficient of 24.0 and the weight of expectation that comes with a Greek club in the Champions Path. The read from the market is not that AEK are much better, it is that neither side is likely to break the other open in a first leg. Second leg in Athens on August 25 or 26.

Champions League Playoff Round FAQ

What is the Champions League playoff round?
It is the final qualifying stage of the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League. Seven league-phase places are decided across two legs, with the first legs on August 18 and 19 and the second legs on August 25 and 26. Winners are determined by aggregate score across the two matches.
Which three playoff ties are played on August 18?
Fenerbahce against Lyon in Istanbul in the League Path, Dinamo Zagreb against Viking in Zagreb in the Champions Path, and Levski Sofia against AEK Athens in Sofia in the Champions Path. All three kick off at 3:00 PM ET.
How did Fenerbahce and Lyon reach the playoff round?
Fenerbahce eliminated Sturm Graz 3-0 on aggregate, closing it out with a 1-0 win in the away leg. Lyon beat Sparta Praha 4-2 on aggregate after winning the home leg 3-0. Both come through the League Path, which is reserved for clubs that did not win their domestic title.
Why is Lyon favoured away from home against Fenerbahce?
DraftKings has Lyon at -125 with Fenerbahce at +400 and the draw at +220, which is an unusual shape for a road side in a two-legged tie. The UEFA club coefficients back it: Lyon enters at 65.750 against Fenerbahce at 57.750, the highest pair of ratings in any of the three ties.
What is the market saying about Levski Sofia and AEK Athens?
The draw is the shortest price on the board at +115, with AEK Athens at +180 and Levski Sofia at +310 in front of their own crowd. Levski carry a UEFA coefficient of 7.0 against 24.0 for AEK, the widest ranking gap of the three ties that still produced a home underdog longer than the draw.
What did Dinamo Zagreb do in the previous round?
Dinamo Zagreb eliminated Kauno Zalgiris 7-1 on aggregate, finishing the job with a 2-1 win in Lithuania. They enter the playoff round with a UEFA coefficient of 46.5 against 8.247 for Viking, and DraftKings prices them at -120 with the draw at +190.

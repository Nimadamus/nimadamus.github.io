Ten Preseason Games, And Nine Of The Ten Spreads Sit Inside Four Points

NFL preseason Week 3, Saturday August 22, 2026, ten games
Published August 22, 2026

Nine of the ten spreads posted for Saturday are 3.5 points or shorter. The exception is Tampa Bay at -5.5 against Kansas City. Eight of the ten totals are the identical number, 36.5, and the two that are not sit one point on either side of it. That is what a market looks like when it has almost no information to price.

First Kickoff Of The Day
Lions TV Network

Commanders at Lions

Saturday, 12:00 PM ET | Ford Field, Detroit, MI

Washington handled Miami 20-7 on August 14, the most convincing margin any team on this board produced in Week 2 outside of Chicago. That result is why the Commanders are the road favourite here at -1.5.

Detroit lost 16-14 in Cincinnati on August 13, a two point game decided late, which is the most common shape a preseason contest takes. The Lions are 0-1 and playing at Ford Field for the first time this August.

The total is 36.5, the number attached to eight of Saturday's ten games. Noon kickoffs in the preseason usually mean an early curtain for the starters, and the group that decides this one is likely to be the group fighting for the last six roster spots.

Lake Erie Afternoon
NFL Network

Bills at Browns

Saturday, 1:00 PM ET | Huntington Bank Field, Cleveland, OH

Cleveland is laying three points at home after being beaten 34-10 by Chicago on August 15, the widest defeat suffered by anyone playing on Saturday. That combination of a bad loss and a home favourite tag is the market pricing depth chart intentions rather than momentum.

Buffalo comes in at 1-0 following a 29-14 win over Carolina on the same day. Twenty nine points was the highest total scored by any Saturday participant in Week 2.

The number is 36.5 again. This is the second of three games kicking off at 1:00 PM ET, and it is the only one of the three where the team that lost by more than three touchdowns is favoured.

Lucas Oil Stadium
CBS4

Falcons at Colts

Saturday, 1:00 PM ET | Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis, IN

Indianapolis owns the only tie on the board. The Colts and New England played to 13-13 on August 13, and that is where the 0-0-1 record comes from. They are 3.5 point home favourites, the joint largest number outside of Tampa Bay.

Atlanta lost 27-7 to Denver on August 14 at home. Seven points was the lowest output of any Saturday participant in Week 2, matched only by Philadelphia and Miami.

The total is 36.5. Indianapolis under a closed roof is a different environment from the outdoor afternoon games elsewhere on this board, and it is the one Saturday matchup where both clubs have something specific to fix on offense.

Two Unbeaten Sides
ESPN / WBAL-TV

Ravens at Vikings

Saturday, 1:00 PM ET | U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis, MN

This is the only game on Saturday between two clubs that both won in Week 2. Baltimore beat Philadelphia 24-7 on August 15 and Minnesota edged the Giants 13-10 on the same afternoon.

Those two wins could not have looked more different. The Ravens produced the largest margin of any team playing Saturday, seventeen points. The Vikings needed a three point margin and scored thirteen. Minnesota is -3 at home.

The total is 36.5. U.S. Bank Stadium is the second indoor venue on the board and, alongside Indianapolis, the environment least likely to interfere with what either coaching staff wants to see from its backups.

SoFi Stadium
ESPN / KCBS / Fox 8

Saints at Rams

Saturday, 4:00 PM ET | SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA

Both of these clubs won in Week 2 and only one of them is favoured. New Orleans beat Jacksonville 24-20 on August 15 and is listed at -1.5 on the road. Los Angeles beat Kansas City 20-12 the same day and is the home underdog.

That Rams win is the most useful data point on this page, because the team they beat plays later on Saturday in Tampa as a 5.5 point underdog. Los Angeles held Kansas City to twelve points and to no touchdown-heavy stretch at any point.

The total is 36.5. The Saints and Rams combined for 44 points in their respective Week 2 games, which is the highest combined figure of any pairing on this board.

Hard Rock Stadium
NFL Network / CBS Miami

Giants at Dolphins

Saturday, 4:00 PM ET | Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens, FL

Two 0-1 clubs, and the visitor is laying 3.5. The Giants lost 13-10 in Minnesota on August 15, a competitive defeat. Miami lost 20-7 in Washington on August 14, and seven points was tied for the lowest total put up by any Saturday participant.

Those two results explain the pricing better than the identical records do. New York was within one score in the fourth quarter. Miami was not.

The total is 36.5. This is the only game on the board where two teams with the same record are separated by more than three points on the spread, which is a small but real signal about how the market read those Week 2 tapes.

Paycor Stadium
Bengals Preseason TV / FOX32

Bears at Bengals

Saturday, 7:00 PM ET | Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati, OH

Chicago produced the most emphatic performance of Week 2 by anyone playing on Saturday, beating Cleveland 34-10 on August 15. Thirty four points was the highest total scored by any club on this board.

Cincinnati beat Detroit 16-14 on August 13 in a game that came down to a single score. The Bengals are 1-0 and hosting, and they are still the underdog at home, with Chicago laying 1.5.

The total is 36.5. Both clubs enter unbeaten, and this is one of only two Saturday matchups where that is true, the other being Baltimore at Minnesota earlier in the day.

Lowest Total On The Board
NFL Network / WBZ

Eagles at Patriots

Saturday, 7:00 PM ET | Gillette Stadium, Foxborough, MA

Thirty five and a half. That is the lowest total anywhere on the Saturday board, and it belongs to the two clubs that scored the fewest points between them in Week 2: Philadelphia managed seven in a 24-7 loss at Baltimore, and New England scored thirteen in its 13-13 tie with Indianapolis.

Twenty combined points across two games is the number driving that total. Philadelphia is still the favourite at -1.5 despite being the only side on this board that lost by seventeen.

New England is 0-0-1 and has not lost yet, which is a technically true statement that means very little. Gillette Stadium in late August is one of the last live looks either staff gets before final roster cuts.

Largest Spread Of The Day
ESPN / KSHB

Chiefs at Buccaneers

Saturday, 7:30 PM ET | Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, FL

Tampa Bay is -5.5, and no other team on Saturday's board is laying more than 3.5. In a preseason week where the market has priced nine of ten games as a coin flip, that number is the one deliberate statement it has made.

The Buccaneers earned it in the ordinary way, beating the Jets 24-16 on August 14 in New Jersey. Kansas City lost 20-12 at home to the Rams on August 15, scoring the second fewest points of any Saturday participant.

The total is 36.5. A 5.5 point preseason spread almost always reflects a decision about playing time rather than a judgement about quality, and the club that has decided to keep its front line units on the field longer is usually the one laying the number.

Last Game Of The Night
NFL Network

Cowboys at Cardinals

Saturday, 10:00 PM ET | State Farm Stadium, Glendale, AZ

Arizona is the only club on this board that has played three preseason games. The Cardinals lost 33-30 to Carolina on August 7 in the earliest game of the entire preseason, then beat Las Vegas 27-14 on August 14, which leaves them at 1-1 while everyone else has one result or fewer.

Dallas beat Seattle 17-7 on August 16, the latest Week 2 game to finish. The Cowboys are 1-0 and are the road underdog here, with Arizona laying 1.5.

The total is 37.5, the highest number on the board. Arizona has scored 57 points across two games, more than double what most Saturday participants have managed, and that is the entire reason this total is a point above the standard 36.5.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many NFL preseason games are on August 22, 2026?
Ten, running from 12:00 PM ET at Ford Field in Detroit to 10:00 PM ET at State Farm Stadium in Glendale. This is preseason Week 3.
What is the largest spread on Saturday's preseason board?
Tampa Bay at -5.5 against Kansas City, kicking off at 7:30 PM ET at Raymond James Stadium. It is the only number on the board larger than 3.5, and the Buccaneers beat the Jets 24-16 in Week 2 while the Chiefs lost 20-12 to the Rams.
What are the totals on the preseason board?
Eight of the ten games are set at 36.5. Philadelphia at New England is the lowest at 35.5 and Dallas at Arizona is the highest at 37.5. Every total on the board sits within two points of every other.
Which teams won in preseason Week 2?
Washington beat Miami 20-7, Buffalo beat Carolina 29-14, Baltimore beat Philadelphia 24-7, Chicago beat Cleveland 34-10, Minnesota beat the Giants 13-10, New Orleans beat Jacksonville 24-20, the Rams beat Kansas City 20-12, Tampa Bay beat the Jets 24-16, Dallas beat Seattle 17-7 and Arizona beat Las Vegas 27-14. Indianapolis and New England tied 13-13.
Why are preseason records unreliable for handicapping?
Because playing time is a coaching decision rather than a competitive one. A 1-0 record in August often reflects how long a starting unit stayed on the field in the second quarter, and coaches change that plan week to week without announcing it. Venue, kickoff time and depth chart intentions carry more information than the results do.

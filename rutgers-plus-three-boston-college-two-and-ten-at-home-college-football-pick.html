1-6 At Home, And The One Win Was Fordham

This is the number the whole ticket rests on.

Strip that game out and the Eagles were 0-6 at Alumni Stadium against FBS opposition, outscored 213 to 114. That is 19.0 points a game scored and 35.5 allowed, at home, over six games.

The Number Is 3, Which Is The Worst Place To Be

Three is the most common final margin in football and it is not close. Sitting exactly on 3 rather than on 3.5 is the least forgiving version of this ticket: a Boston College win by exactly a field goal is a push and the money comes back, and anything more than that loses.

It is worth being clear about that rather than glossing it. The half point is not owned here. What is owned is every Rutgers win and every one point or two point Boston College win.

The Argument Against

Rutgers lost at home to Massachusetts. There is no spin available on this. UMass came to Piscataway and won by 16 nine days ago. A team that loses that game does not get to be treated as the reliable side of anything.

27 rushing yards. That is what Rutgers produced on the ground in the opener, and 319 of its 346 yards came through the air. A one dimensional offense on the road in September in New England is a thin thing to lay 2 units on.

Rutgers was 1-4 on the road last season. The five wins were built at home. Away from Piscataway the Scarlet Knights beat Purdue by 3 and lost to Minnesota, Washington, Illinois and Ohio State by a combined 77 points.

Last season is last season. Boston College's 2-10 was a different roster and a different year, and a market that has seen both openers has moved on from it. The 3 is the market saying the 2025 records do not apply. This ticket is the argument that they still do.

Bottom Line

A team that went 2-10 with a -88 differential, that won exactly one home game all season and needed an FCS opponent to do it, is laying three points to a team that finished six games ahead of it.

Week one gave the market a reason to reprice both of them and the yardage in both games says week one was mostly turnovers. Take the points with the club that was demonstrably better across twelve games.

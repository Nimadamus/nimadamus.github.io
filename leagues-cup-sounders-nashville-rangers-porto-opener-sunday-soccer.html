Soccer - Sunday, August 9

Lumen Field Opens A Sunday That Runs From Kilmarnock To The Allianz Parque

The Leagues Cup puts eight ties on American fields tonight, and the first of them kicks at Lumen Field in the middle of the afternoon. Before that the Scottish Premiership plays four fixtures, with Rangers hosting Hibernian at Ibrox and Celtic taking a perfect record to Kilmarnock. FC Porto opens its Primeira Liga season in Portugal, and in Brazil the runaway league leaders host the club sitting seventeenth. Analysis only, no picks.

Marquee Match
Leagues Cup

Queretaro at Seattle Sounders

Sunday, 3:30 PM ET | Lumen Field, Seattle, WA

The first of eight Leagues Cup ties on American soil kicks in Seattle, and it is a study in two clubs arriving from opposite directions. Queretaro sits seventh in the Liga MX table three matches into the campaign at 2-0-1 with five goals scored and four conceded. Seattle is 7-3-7 in Major League Soccer, ninth in the Western Conference on 24 points.

Seattle's season has been defined by an offense that will not fire. The Sounders have scored 20 goals in 17 matches, the lowest total of any club in the Western Conference, while conceding 22. That is a goal difference of minus two attached to a team that has lost only seven times, which is the signature of a side drawing games it should win.

Queretaro's early Liga MX form has been tidier than its reputation suggests: two wins from three, and only four goals conceded. The venue is the variable. Lumen Field is an NFL stadium and Seattle plays its Leagues Cup home ties there, which gives a club scoring 1.18 goals a match in league play the loudest environment it will see all summer.

Best Defense In MLS
Leagues Cup

Atletico San Luis at Nashville SC

Sunday, 8:00 PM ET | GEODIS Park, Nashville, TN

Nashville leads the Eastern Conference on 40 points from 18 matches at 12-4-2, and the number that explains it is 14. That is the total number of goals the club has conceded all season, the fewest in Major League Soccer by a clear margin. Thirty-five scored, fourteen allowed, a plus 21 difference.

Atletico de San Luis arrives in the opposite condition. Three matches into the Liga MX season the club has no wins, sitting fifteenth on two points from two draws and a defeat with four goals scored and five conceded.

The tactical question is whether a Liga MX side that has not yet found a result can break down the most organized defensive structure in the North American game on its own field. Nashville have taken 40 of a possible 54 points across 18 matches, and the Leagues Cup carries CONCACAF Champions Cup places, so there is no version of this where the home side rests its shape.

A Season Gone Wrong
Leagues Cup

Necaxa at Philadelphia Union

Sunday, 7:30 PM ET | Subaru Park, Chester, PA

Philadelphia is having the season nobody in Chester expected. The Union are 4-4-10 for 16 points, thirteenth in the Eastern Conference, with 25 goals scored and 33 conceded. Ten defeats in eighteen matches, and only Atlanta and CF Montreal have taken fewer points in the East.

Necaxa comes in eighth in Liga MX at 2-0-1 with six points from three matches, five goals scored and five conceded. It is a small sample but a positive one, and the Mexican side arrives without the weight of a collapsing league campaign.

For Philadelphia this tournament is the only competition left where the season can be salvaged, which changes how a manager picks a team in August. Subaru Park has been the one place the Union have looked like themselves, and a knockout format removes the accumulated damage of a bad league table.

Border Derby At Snapdragon
Leagues Cup

Tijuana at San Diego FC

Sunday, 10:00 PM ET | Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego, CA

An international border separates these two clubs and the Leagues Cup has put them on the same field. Tijuana is second in Liga MX at 2-0-1 with seven points, four goals scored and one conceded, the joint-best defensive record in the early table. San Diego FC is 5-6-7 in Major League Soccer, thirteenth in the West on 21 points.

The expansion side has been more entertaining than effective: 32 goals scored and 29 conceded in 18 matches, which is the most attacking output of any club in the bottom half of the Western Conference. San Diego creates chances and gives them straight back.

Tijuana has conceded once in three league matches. If that defensive shape travels across the border, this becomes a test of whether San Diego's front line can find the one goal its own defense will probably need to be enough. The nightcap of the American schedule gets the geography it deserves.

Third Matchday At Ibrox
Scottish Premiership

Hibernian at Rangers

Sunday, 11:00 AM ET | Ibrox Stadium, Glasgow

Two matchdays into the Scottish Premiership season, Rangers have one point and Hibernian have none. Rangers sit eighth on a single draw from their opening fixture. Hibernian are eleventh after losing their opener 2-1. Neither start is what either club planned.

Ibrox is the reason this is not a level fight on paper. Rangers have played once, drawn 1-1 and now get their first home league fixture of the campaign against a side that has already lost at home.

Hibernian's problem is that a single fixture is too small a sample to know anything, and their one match produced a defeat in which they did score. In a division where Celtic and St Mirren have both opened with two wins, dropping further points here would leave Rangers five behind the leaders before the end of the month.

Two Wins, One Goal Conceded
Scottish Premiership

Celtic at Kilmarnock

Sunday, 8:30 AM ET | The BBSP Stadium, Kilmarnock

Celtic top the Scottish Premiership with six points from two matches, six goals scored and one conceded. That is the maximum available and the best goal difference in the division. The champions have started exactly the way champions are supposed to.

Kilmarnock are bottom. Two matches, two defeats, zero points, and a goals-against column reading nine, which is the worst in the league by four. They have scored four, so the attack is functional. The defense has not been.

This is the first away test of Celtic's season and it comes against the one side in the division currently incapable of keeping a clean sheet. The interesting question is not the result but the margin, and whether Kilmarnock can find any structure at all after conceding nine goals in two matches.

Season Opener At The Dragao
Primeira Liga

Alverca at FC Porto

Sunday, 1:00 PM ET | Estadio do Dragao, Porto

FC Porto have not played a Primeira Liga match yet this season. Neither have Benfica, Braga, Gil Vicente or Moreirense, all of whom open today. The first matchday of the Portuguese season has been spread across the weekend and the heavyweight fixtures fall on Sunday.

Alverca are the visitors and they too have zero matches played, which means this preview has no form to lean on and will not pretend otherwise. What is verifiable is the setting: the Estadio do Dragao, a full house for a season opener, and a Porto side beginning its campaign a week after Sporting CP dropped two points in a 2-2 draw on matchday one.

That last detail matters more than it looks. Sporting have already dropped points before Porto have kicked a ball, so a home win today would put the Dragao side two clear of one of its title rivals on the opening Sunday.

Brazil's Runaway Leaders
Brazilian Serie A

Internacional at Palmeiras

Sunday, 3:00 PM ET | Allianz Parque, Sao Paulo

Palmeiras lead the Brazilian Serie A with 47 points from 21 matches at 14-5-2, eight clear of Flamengo. Thirty-eight goals scored, sixteen conceded, the best defensive record in the division. This is a title campaign running at full speed in the middle of August.

Internacional are seventeenth. Five wins, seven draws, nine defeats, 22 points from 21 matches, and a goal difference of minus four. In a twenty-team league where four go down, that is relegation territory with a third of the season left.

Internacional's problem has not been losing heavily. Seven draws in twenty-one matches is the shape of a side that keeps games close and cannot finish them, and 23 goals in 21 outings is the reason. Palmeiras have conceded 16 all season.

Eight and zero is not a large sample and nobody should pretend it is. It is also the only road sample either of these teams has.

Seven Months Ago They Lost By 16

This is the part of the page that has to come before the case, not after it.

On February 8, 2026, Seattle beat New England 29 to 13 in Super Bowl LX. Sixteen points. That is more than four times this spread, and it is the single most recent piece of evidence anybody has about how these two rosters match up.

The road to it was not soft on either side. Seattle went through San Francisco 41 to 6 and the Rams 31 to 27. New England went through the Chargers 16 to 3, Houston 28 to 16 and Denver 10 to 7. Those three Patriots wins produced 54 points in total, which is a very different offense from the 28.82 a game it averaged in the regular season.

If you think the Super Bowl was the real reading and the regular season was the noise, this ticket is a bad idea and there is no argument on this page that fixes that.

Why 3.5 And Not 3

Three is the most common margin in professional football and it is not close. Every half point that carries a number across 3 is worth more than every other half point on the board, which is why +3.5 costs -115 and +3 would be sitting near even money or better.

What that buys is the outcome where Seattle wins by exactly a field goal. On +3 that is a push and the money comes back. On +3.5 it is a winner.

Given two teams that finished with identical records and point differentials 21 apart across seventeen games, a game decided by exactly three points is not an edge case. It is the single most likely margin on the board.

The Argument Against

Seattle was better by the numbers that usually matter. A plus 191 differential against plus 170, and 17.18 points a game allowed against 18.82. Over seventeen games that is a real gap and it points at the home side.

Lumen Field is a genuine home field. Seattle scored 30.6 a game there. Crowd noise in that building is not a broadcast talking point, it is a documented false start problem for visiting offenses.

The most recent meeting was a rout. Sixteen points, on a neutral field, with both teams healthy and prepared. Nothing on this page outranks that as evidence.

Week 1 is the least knowable week of the year. Neither club has played a snap that counts since February. Every number here is seven months old.

What Week 1 Numbers Are Worth

Worth saying plainly: every figure on this page is from the 2025 season, because the 2026 season starts tonight and there is nothing else. No 2026 statistic exists for either team.

That is not a disclaimer, it is the actual condition of the bet. In week 1 the market is pricing last year's teams plus an offseason of roster movement that nobody has seen play together. The people telling you they know what these teams are in September are working from the same seventeen games as everybody else.

What survives that uncertainty better than anything else is a repeated structural fact, and the one on this page is that New England did not lose a road game across a full season.

Bottom Line

Two teams that both went 14-3, separated by 21 points of differential across seventeen games, and the one getting 3.5 points is the one that went 8-0 on the road at 29.5 points a game.

The counter is the biggest one there is: Seattle beat this exact team by 16 in the Super Bowl seven months ago, at 17.18 points a game allowed on the season, and it is at home.

At -115 this needs to be right 53.49 percent of the time. Between two clubs with identical records, in the sport where 3 is the most common margin, with the point through 3 already bought, that is the number I am willing to take.

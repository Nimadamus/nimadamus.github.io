Liverpool Open Preseason Against Sunderland While MLS Plays A Full Saturday

The marquee name on the board carries no league points: Liverpool open their preseason tour against Sunderland at GEODIS Park in Nashville. Major League Soccer fills out the rest of the day, headlined by Inter Miami's trip to CF Montreal without Lionel Messi and a red-hot LAFC side hosting a struggling Sporting Kansas City club at BMO Stadium. Form, venues, and market context for each below.

Liverpool vs Sunderland

6:00 PM ET | GEODIS Park, Nashville, TN | Club Friendly

Club FriendlySummer Soccer SeriesNashville

Liverpool's preseason begins in earnest at GEODIS Park, the 30,109-seat home of Nashville SC, as part of TEG Sport's 2026 Summer Soccer Series. This is Liverpool's opening match of the tour, and with newly promoted Sunderland providing a genuine Premier League opponent rather than a club or academy side, the fixture carries more football relevance than a typical July friendly. Expect a hot, humid night: forecasts call for temperatures near 28 degrees Celsius that will feel closer to 37 with the local humidity, with a chance of lightning delays around kickoff.

Sunderland enter the match having secured their return to the Premier League and treat the trip to Nashville as valuable exposure against elite opposition before their own top-flight campaign begins. For Liverpool, the priorities are conditioning, integrating squad additions, and building match sharpness after the offseason layoff. No competitive stakes are attached, but for a global fanbase this is the first live look at the group ahead of the new campaign, and it is the clear headline of the day's soccer board even without three points on the line.

The match is the opener in a wider run of high-profile preseason friendlies hitting American stadiums this summer, with several more international club fixtures scheduled at NFL and MLS venues over the following weeks. Kickoff is 11:00 PM local time in England, meaning most of Liverpool's domestic fanbase will be watching in the late evening, while the 6:00 PM ET Nashville slot puts the match in front of a live American crowd that rarely gets a top-four Premier League club this early in a preseason cycle. Both clubs are expected to rotate heavily given the heat, with a full ninety minutes of testing conditions likely to shape substitution patterns as much as tactics.

MLSMatchday 18Messi Absent

Inter Miami arrive at Stade Saputo without Lionel Messi, who is sitting out as part of a rest period after leading Argentina to the 2026 FIFA World Cup final, along with Rodrigo De Paul. New signing Casemiro, announced as a free-agent addition earlier in the week, is also not expected to feature. In their place, Luis Suarez leads the attack for a Miami side that has won five straight and sits second in the Eastern Conference at 10-2-4 for 34 points.

CF Montreal are in the opposite spot, having lost 11 of their last 20 league games and coming off a 1-0 defeat to Nashville. They sit four points outside the Eastern Conference play-in positions and badly need a result in front of their own fans to keep their postseason hopes alive. Even without its biggest name, Miami's five-game winning streak and superior form make this a clear gap in quality, and Montreal's inconsistent form this season does little to suggest they can close it.

LAFC vs Sporting Kansas City

10:40 PM ET | BMO Stadium, Los Angeles, CA | MLS Regular Season
Kickoff
10:40 PM ET

LAFC 3rd, SKC 15th

MLSWestern ConferenceBMO Stadium

LAFC have won three straight, including a 3-0 win at rival LA Galaxy and a 3-1 home win over Real Salt Lake, and sit third in the Western Conference at 9-5-3. Goalkeeper Hugo Lloris, at 39 years old, has already recorded nine shutouts this season, and Son Heung-min leads all of MLS in assists with 10, giving LAFC both the league's stingiest defense and one of its most productive creative players.

Sporting Kansas City visit sitting 15th in the West at 4-10-2, though they arrive on a mild upswing after a 2-1 win over Minnesota United in their last outing, going 2-2-1 across their last five. Dejan Joveljic leads Sporting's attack with eight goals in 16 games, enough to make them a threat in transition, but LAFC's defensive record and home form make them the clear favorite. A predictive model gives LAFC a 76.4 percent win probability against a 9.6 percent chance for Sporting Kansas City and a 14.0 percent draw.

The gap between the two clubs' seasons has been stark. LAFC's front office built a roster around defensive solidity, and it shows: nine shutouts through the season is a total most Western Conference clubs would take across two full campaigns, and pairing that with Son's league-leading assist total gives Los Angeles both ends of the field covered. Sporting Kansas City's recent form offers a sliver of hope that the club has found something workable in Joveljic's finishing, but climbing out of 15th place in the West against a team playing this well at home is a tall order for a Saturday night kickoff at BMO Stadium.

Soccer Wednesday: Four Champions League Second Legs And Not One Of Them Is Decided

Nine matches, and the four that matter most are all unresolved. Every one of the Champions League playoff second legs kicking off today is level on aggregate: Lyon and Fenerbahce at 1-1, Viking and Dinamo Zagreb at 2-2, Slovan Bratislava and NK Celje at 1-1, and Levski Sofia and AEK Athens at 0-0. Real Madrid host Real Sociedad at the Bernabeu at -525, and the Carabao Cup adds four ties including the closest price of the day at Valley Parade.

The tie of the day. Fenerbahce and Lyon drew 1-1 in Istanbul on August 18 and the whole thing now comes down to ninety minutes in France, with a Champions League league phase place on the other side of it.

The market cannot separate them either. Lyon are -105 at home with the draw at +250 and the total set at 2.5, which is a book saying home advantage is worth almost exactly nothing here.

Form says the same thing from a different angle. Lyon come in on WDWLL across their last five and Fenerbahce on WDLWW. Two sides in identical shape, level on aggregate, playing for the same prize.

Nothing at all happened in Sofia. Levski and AEK played out a 0-0 on August 18, which means the second leg in Athens is effectively a one off match with extra time waiting behind it.

AEK are -215 at the OPAP Arena with the draw at +320 and the total at 2.5. That price is the clearest expression of home advantage on this board, and it is being asked to do a lot of work after a first leg in which neither side scored.

The loudest first leg of the four. Dinamo Zagreb and Viking traded goals to a 2-2 in Croatia on August 18, and the Norwegian side go home level with two away goals already banked in a competition that no longer counts them for anything.

Viking are +135 at home, which is the only home underdog price among the four second legs. The draw is +260 and the total is 2.5, a number that looks low given what happened in the first leg.

Dinamo are the steadier side by form at DWWWD against Viking on DLWWD, and they are the club with the European pedigree. The tie is still level and it is being played in Stavanger.

Real Madrid host Real Sociedad at the Bernabeu at -525, the shortest price on the entire board across every sport today. The draw is +650 and the total is 3.5.

Madrid have won four of their last five and opened the LaLiga season with a win. Sociedad have not won any of their last five, going LDLDD, and start the campaign without a victory.

Benfica On The Brink And Sudamericana Second Legs Headline A Thursday Of Soccer

Benfica walk into the Estádio da Luz trailing Swiss side St. Gallen 2-1 in Europa League qualifying, Boca Juniors defend a one-goal lead in Rancagua, Grêmio try to overturn a deficit against Bolívar with their league season in the relegation zone, Corinthians host third-place Athletico-PR in the Brasileirão, and Sunderland meet Leeds United in a Premier League preseason friendly at Red Bull Arena. Analysis only, no picks.

Europa League Qualifying
3:00 PM ET

Benfica vs St. Gallen

Thursday, 3:00 PM ET | Estádio da Luz, Lisbon, Portugal

A week ago in Switzerland, St. Gallen did something almost nobody priced in: they beat Benfica 2-1 and now carry a one-goal aggregate lead into the Estádio da Luz. That result is why a routine qualifying return leg has become the most watchable game of Thursday's slate. Benfica have played European knockout football for decades, and losing a qualifying tie to a Swiss Super League side would be a genuine embarrassment for a club of this size, which is exactly the tension the second leg carries.

Oddsmakers have no doubt about the response. DraftKings opened Benfica at -525 to win the night and the price has been hammered all the way to -900, with the draw at +800 and St. Gallen out at +2000 after opening +1000. That is a violent move toward the hosts, and the total sitting at 3.5 says oddsmakers expect Benfica to attack from the opening whistle rather than manage the game. Benfica's recent form line shows two wins, two draws and one defeat across their last five, that single defeat being the first leg itself, while St. Gallen have won three of their last five.

The math is simple and unforgiving. With the away-goals rule long gone from UEFA competition, a 1-0 Benfica win levels the aggregate at 2-2 and forces extra time in Lisbon, while any win by two or more sends Benfica through in ninety minutes. St. Gallen can advance with a draw, which invites the Swiss side to sit deep and make Benfica break down a packed defensive block for ninety minutes. Chasing games against a bus parked in front of goal is a skill of its own, and how quickly Benfica find the first goal will decide whether this becomes a rout or a full-blown crisis.

Copa Sudamericana R16
6:00 PM ET

Grêmio vs Bolívar

Thursday, 6:00 PM ET | Arena do Grêmio, Porto Alegre, Brazil

Bolívar beat Grêmio 3-2 in the first leg of this Copa Sudamericana round of 16 tie a week ago in La Paz, where the Bolivian club has punished visitors at altitude for generations. Now the tie comes down to sea level in Porto Alegre, and DraftKings prices Grêmio at -350 on the night, with the draw at +450 and Bolívar at +900, a striking line for a team that lost the first meeting and has not won any of its last five matches.

That form line is not a blip. Grêmio sit seventeenth in the Brasileirão table at 22 points from 20 matches, inside the relegation places, with five wins, seven draws and eight losses in league play. Their last five results across competitions read as four draws and losses without a single victory, which makes the Sudamericana the one live route to salvaging this season. Bolívar arrive in far better rhythm, with three wins and a draw in their last five, and they showed in the first leg they can score against this Grêmio defense three times in one night.

Simple arithmetic gives the night its shape: Grêmio must win by two clear goals to take the tie outright in ninety minutes, a one-goal win only levels the aggregate at 5-5, and any draw or better sends Bolívar through. The total of 2.5 is a modest number for a tie that produced five goals in the first leg, and it suggests the market expects Bolívar to defend the lead rather than trade punches away from altitude. A team that cannot buy a win domestically now needs a multi-goal performance in its own stadium with the season's most realistic prize on the line.

Brasileirão Série A
6:30 PM ET

Corinthians vs Athletico-PR

Thursday, 6:30 PM ET | Neo Química Arena, São Paulo, Brazil

On paper this is the table's mismatch flowing the wrong way. Athletico-PR sit third in the Brasileirão at 36 points, eight clear of ninth-place Corinthians, and arrive on a five-match unbeaten run of four straight wins and a draw. Yet DraftKings makes Corinthians the -105 favorite at the Neo Química Arena, with the draw at +210 and the visitors at +340. Home advantage in São Paulo is being priced as worth more than a thirteen-place gap in the standings.

The underlying numbers explain some of the respect for Athletico. Through 20 matches they have scored 28 and conceded just 19, a +9 goal difference built on eleven wins against three draws and six defeats, and that defensive record travels well in a league where road points are scarce. Corinthians have been the definition of mid-table at 7-7-6 with 22 goals scored and 20 allowed, though their recent form of three wins in the last five, including results either side of a single defeat, is better than their season-long numbers.

The total tells you what kind of night the market expects: 1.5, one of the lowest numbers you will see on a professional match. Two organized defenses, a visiting side that grinds out narrow results, and a home team that rarely wins by volume all point toward a game decided by a single moment. For Athletico, a road win here would tighten their grip on a top-three position; for Corinthians, beating a direct quality test at home is the kind of result that starts a climb from ninth toward the continental places.

Preseason Friendly
7:30 PM ET

Sunderland vs Leeds United

Thursday, 7:30 PM ET | Red Bull Arena, Harrison, NJ

Two Premier League clubs meet on American soil as Sunderland and Leeds United bring their preseason preparations to Red Bull Arena in New Jersey. These are two of English football's great traditional names, both with enormous traveling support and both coming off seasons that could hardly have felt more different. Sunderland finished seventh in the Premier League with 54 points, a remarkable campaign that had them in the conversation for European places, while Leeds finished fourteenth at 47 points, safe but never comfortable.

Friendlies in July are about minutes, fitness and auditions rather than results, and that is the honest frame for this one. Managers typically run out separate elevens by half, trialists and academy players get stretches against real opposition, and the scoreline matters less than who looks sharp ahead of the 2026-27 season opener. What makes this fixture better than the average summer exhibition is that these squads will see each other again when the real thing starts, so there is a scouting edge buried inside the exercise.

For American fans in the New York area, this is also simply a rare product: a genuine all-English matchup, at a proper soccer stadium, between two clubs whose league meetings carry real stakes. Northeast supporters clubs for both sides are substantial, and a Thursday evening kickoff at Red Bull Arena should draw a crowd that sounds far more like a league fixture than an exhibition. No betting lines are posted for this one, which is standard for preseason friendlies, and the analysis here is about form and personnel rather than any market.

Copa Sudamericana R16
8:30 PM ET

O'Higgins vs Boca Juniors

Thursday, 8:30 PM ET | Estadio El Teniente, Rancagua, Chile

Boca Juniors take a 1-0 aggregate lead into the Chilean mountains after beating O'Higgins by that score at home in Buenos Aires last week, and the second leg of this Copa Sudamericana round of 16 tie at Estadio El Teniente closes Thursday's board. One goal is the narrowest lead a team can defend, and Boca will spend ninety minutes in Rancagua protecting it against a home side with nothing to lose.

Betting markets have moved against the Argentine giants. DraftKings opened Boca at -105 to win the match and the price has drifted to +100, with O'Higgins out from +275 to +300 and the draw at +225. A drifting favorite in a road second leg usually reflects an expectation of pragmatism: Boca do not need to win this game, they need to not lose it, and their recent form of two wins, two losses and a draw across the last five does not suggest a team that will chase goals unnecessarily. O'Higgins have been up and down as well, with two wins, two losses and a draw in the same span.

The scenarios are clean. Any draw sends Boca through 1-0 on aggregate, an O'Higgins win by exactly one levels the tie, and a two-goal home win takes it outright. The total of 2.5 leans low for a match where one side must score to survive, which says the market believes Boca can strangle the tempo the way road teams protecting leads have done in this competition for years. For a club of Boca's stature, the Sudamericana knockout rounds are an expected destination; for O'Higgins, eliminating Boca Juniors over two legs would be one of the biggest continental results in the club's history.

Xabi Alonso's Chelsea Debut Headlines A Tuesday Of Preseason Soccer

Chelsea's new manager gets his first look in front of fans against Western Sydney Wanderers in the Sydney Super Cup, David Moyes brings an unbeaten Everton to Staffordshire to face Stoke, and Pierre Sage gets an emotional reunion with former club Lens at the Como Cup on the shores of Lake Como. Sydney Super Cup - Game One
10:45 AM BST

Chelsea vs Western Sydney Wanderers

Tuesday, 7:45 PM local (10:45 AM BST) | Accor Stadium, Sydney, Australia

This is the one everyone in Sydney wants to see: Chelsea's first game in front of paying fans under new manager Xabi Alonso, opening the inaugural Sydney Super Cup against a Western Sydney Wanderers side that arrives full of confidence. The Wanderers just beat Tigers FC 2-0 in the Round of 32 of the Australia Cup, giving them a competitive edge that most of Chelsea's preseason opponents will not have carried into a friendly.

Chelsea has already played a pair of behind-closed-doors friendlies back in England, beating both Crawley Town and Bromley, but Tuesday marks the first time supporters get an actual look at how Alonso wants his team to play. Cole Palmer is expected to feature along with Brazilian pair Joao Pedro and Estevao, giving Alonso a chance to start building combinations among his most talented attacking pieces ahead of the new Premier League season.

For Western Sydney, this is as big a stage as their club gets outside of A-League finals, a chance to test a full-strength Chelsea side rather than another domestic opponent. The Wanderers' recent cup form suggests they will not simply be making up the numbers, even against a club with a squad several multiples more valuable than their own.

The match is also the opening fixture of a tournament running from July 28 through August 12 that brings together four clubs with genuinely different profiles: the Wanderers and fellow Australian side Sydney FC alongside two Premier League heavyweights in Chelsea and Tottenham Hotspur. For Australian fans, having both English clubs and both major Sydney sides in the same competition is a rare chance to measure the gap directly rather than through television. For Alonso, it is the first of several matches in the window to start settling on a first-choice shape before the real fixtures count.

Preseason Friendly
7:45 PM BST

Stoke City vs Everton

Tuesday, 7:45 PM BST | bet365 Stadium, Stoke-on-Trent, England

Stoke's preseason has been a mixed bag so far, a 1-0 defeat to Braga followed by a draw with Crewe Alexandra and then a win over Chester, one win, one draw, one loss heading into Tuesday. Everton arrives in far better form, unbeaten through two friendlies under David Moyes, a 4-0 win over Dundee followed by a goalless draw against Bolton Wanderers.

Moyes has several new faces to work into the group. Merlin Röhl's loan move has been made permanent, while Tyrique George arrives from Chelsea and Hayden Hackney joins from Middlesbrough, both looking to make an early impression before the Premier League season starts. The projected Everton XI (Travers; O'Brien, Tarkowski, Keane, Mykolenko; Röhl, Armstrong, Iroegbunam, Dewsbury-Hall, George; Beto) gives an early look at how the new arrivals might slot in around the existing spine of the team.

For Stoke, still working through the Championship rhythm of preseason, games like this against Premier League opposition are as much about identifying rotation pieces as about the result. A composed performance against a mostly first-choice Everton side would be a meaningful marker regardless of the final score.

Como Cup - Group A
6:00 PM BST

Crystal Palace vs Lens

Tuesday, 6:00 PM BST | Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia, Lake Como, Italy

There is a personal storyline running through this one: Crystal Palace manager Pierre Sage takes on his former club Lens for the first time since leaving, in a Como Cup group stage with an unusual format, each side plays two 45-minute matches, with any draws settled by penalty shootout rather than extra time. Palace are looking to bounce back after a loss in their last outing, while Lens are trying to protect an unbeaten preseason record.

Lens brings real attacking quality to Lake Como in Florian Thauvin and Thorgan Hazard, two experienced forwards who represent the toughest defensive test Palace will have faced so far this preseason. For a Sage side still finding its identity heading into the new campaign, containing that pair for a full 90 minutes across two halves of competitive intensity will say more about where this Palace team stands than the eventual scoreline.

The picturesque Giuseppe Sinigaglia Stadium setting aside, both clubs are using the Como Cup format's split-match structure to get extended runouts for squad players who might not see 90 minutes in a traditional friendly, which should mean rotation through both 45-minute periods and a wider look at each roster than a single result would suggest.

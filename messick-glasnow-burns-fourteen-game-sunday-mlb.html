MLB - Sunday, August 30

Fourteen Games, Four Starters Under A 3.10 ERA, And Max Scherzer At 6.62

Major League Baseball, Sunday August 30, 2026, fourteen games
Published August 30, 2026

Four men take the ball this afternoon with an ERA under 3.10 and a full season of innings behind it: Drew Rasmussen at 2.88, Chase Burns at 2.77, Parker Messick at 2.54 and Tyler Glasnow at 3.02 on a much shorter workload. A fifth, Max Scherzer, walks out at Rogers Centre carrying a 6.62. Fourteen games, no doubleheaders, and the spread between the best and worst starting line on the board is the widest it has been all week.

The Early Window

Marlins at Nationals

Sunday, 12:15 PM ET | Nationals Park, Washington, DC

The first pitch of the day and the widest run environment gap of the early window. Washington is 65-73 and has scored 719 runs in 139 games, the most of any club on this board. Miami is 68-68 and has allowed 578 in 137 behind a 3.90 staff ERA, which is the better half of the league.

Janson Junk starts for Miami at 6-8 with a 4.11 ERA, a 1.30 WHIP and a .266 opponent average across 103 innings. He is a contact pitcher in a season of strikeout pitchers, 6.12 per nine, the second lowest figure among today’s fourteen starters.

Andrew Alvarez counters at 2-6 with a 3.47 ERA and a 1.31 WHIP over 90.2 innings, a considerably better line than his record suggests and the reason is behind him: Washington has allowed 698 runs and carries a 4.63 staff ERA, worst of any club playing before four o’clock. No number was posted for this game at publication.

A 5.52 Staff ERA In Atlanta

Rockies at Braves

Sunday, 1:35 PM ET | Truist Park, Atlanta, GA

Colorado at 52-84 brings the worst pitching staff in the sport to a park that does not forgive it. The Rockies have allowed 785 runs in 137 games with a 5.52 ERA, a 1.52 WHIP and a .288 opponent average, every one of those the poorest mark on today’s board. Atlanta is 81-55 and has allowed 517.

Mason Adams gets the ball for Colorado with seven major league innings to his name, a 3.86 ERA and two starts. Seven innings is the smallest sample of any pitcher scheduled today and there is essentially nothing in it to read.

Tyler Mahle answers at 5-10 with a 4.28 ERA, a 1.30 WHIP and 8.56 strikeouts per nine across 122 innings. Atlanta hits .248 with a .724 OPS, which is unremarkable, and the case for the Braves here has nothing to do with their bats. No price was posted at publication.

The Best Two Staffs On The Board

Red Sox at Yankees

Sunday, 1:35 PM ET | Yankee Stadium, Bronx, NY

The two best run prevention clubs on this fourteen game board, playing each other, with the division between them. New York is 77-59 with a 3.22 staff ERA and 504 runs allowed in 137 games. Boston is 74-62 at 3.47 and 509. Nobody else on today’s slate is under 3.55.

Ranger Suarez starts for Boston at 5-3 with a 3.26 ERA, a 1.20 WHIP and a .234 opponent average across 121.1 innings and 24 starts. He misses a fair number of bats at 8.75 per nine without ever having been a power arm, which is the profile that ages best.

Will Warren answers at 8-6 with a 4.29 ERA and a 1.40 WHIP over 130 innings, the weaker of the two lines, though his 9.07 strikeouts per nine is the higher figure. The Yankees hit .230 as a club, the lowest average of any team playing today, and win anyway on the strength of the run prevention. No number was posted at publication.

Gilbert Against A 6.62 ERA

Mariners at Blue Jays

Sunday, 1:37 PM ET | Rogers Centre, Toronto, ON

Two clubs under .500 and the widest starter gap on the board. Seattle is 64-72 and has scored 530 runs in 137 games with a .681 OPS, the lowest offensive figure of any team playing today. Toronto is 67-70 and has scored 543 with a .688.

Logan Gilbert is having the quiet excellent season nobody outside the Pacific Northwest is discussing. Eleven and seven, a 3.42 ERA, a 1.03 WHIP and a .212 opponent average across 158 innings and 27 starts, with 9.40 strikeouts per nine. Only one starter on this board has thrown more innings.

Max Scherzer takes the ball for Toronto at 1-7 with a 6.62 ERA and a 1.47 WHIP across 53 innings in thirteen starts. The name still moves a room. The 2026 line is the worst of any starter working today by nearly two full runs of ERA over anyone with more than forty innings. No price was posted at publication.

A Rookie At 2.54

Royals at Guardians

Sunday, 1:40 PM ET | Progressive Field, Cleveland, OH

Kansas City at 62-75 beat Cleveland 8-3 in this ballpark yesterday, which is the context every number below is fighting. The Guardians are 68-68 and have allowed 559 runs in 137 games with 1,266 strikeouts, the second highest total on this board.

Parker Messick is the story. The rookie left hander is 10-8 with a 2.54 ERA, a 1.03 WHIP and a .209 opponent average across 156 innings and 27 starts, striking out 9.06 per nine. Among the fourteen men starting today only Drew Rasmussen has a lower ERA, and nobody has been more durable at that level of run prevention.

Seth Lugo counters at 6-7 with a 4.60 ERA, a 1.38 WHIP and a .279 opponent average over 150.2 innings, the highest opponent average of any starter working before four o’clock. Kansas City has allowed 662 runs behind a 4.63 staff ERA. No number was posted at publication.

Glasnow And Valdez

Dodgers at Tigers

Sunday, 1:40 PM ET | Comerica Park, Detroit, MI

Los Angeles is 81-55 and has scored 664 runs behind a .757 team OPS, the best offensive figure on this board. Detroit is 63-72 and has allowed 525 runs with a 3.56 ERA, the third best mark playing today. Those two facts point in opposite directions and the number in this building is going to sit between them.

Tyler Glasnow brings the most extreme line of the day. Three and zero, a 3.02 ERA, a 0.87 WHIP and a .169 opponent average with 11.28 strikeouts per nine. Nobody starting today allows fewer baserunners and nobody misses more bats. He has also thrown 44.2 innings across eight starts, the second smallest workload on the board.

Framber Valdez answers at 8-9 with a 4.35 ERA, a 1.41 WHIP and a .261 opponent average across 144.2 innings and 26 starts. The ERA overstates the problem in a park that has suppressed run scoring all year, but 1.41 baserunners an inning against the deepest lineup in the National League is a real difficulty. No price was posted at publication.

Two Sub 3.15 Starters

Padres at Rays

Sunday, 1:40 PM ET | Tropicana Field, St. Petersburg, FL

The best pitching matchup on the board by combined quality and it is being played by two clubs already looking at October. Tampa Bay is 81-54 and hits .261 as a team, the highest average of anyone playing today. San Diego is 72-64 and has allowed 568 runs.

Drew Rasmussen has the lowest ERA of any starter working today at 2.88, with a 0.93 WHIP, a .202 opponent average and a 14-5 record across 143.2 innings and 26 starts. That is the rare combination of a full season workload and elite rate stats, and the Rays sit where they do largely because of it.

Robbie Ray is 11-7 with a 3.14 ERA, a 1.31 WHIP and a .226 opponent average over 146 innings. The WHIP is the soft spot, a full third of a baserunner an inning worse than Rasmussen, and San Diego hits .237 as a club, so the margin for a walk heavy afternoon is thin. No number was posted at publication.

The Loosest Game On The Board

White Sox at Twins

Sunday, 2:10 PM ET | Target Field, Minneapolis, MN

If you are looking for runs today, this is where the numbers say they are. Minnesota is 64-72 and has allowed 681 runs behind a 4.68 ERA, the worst mark of any club playing indoors or out this afternoon outside Colorado and the Athletics. Chicago is 72-63 and has scored 651, the second most of anyone on the board.

Zebby Matthews starts for the Twins at 8-8 with a 4.82 ERA, a 1.30 WHIP and a .260 opponent average across 104.2 innings in nineteen starts. Nothing in that line suggests a shutdown afternoon.

Jordan Hicks takes the ball for Chicago with a 3.76 ERA that hides a 1.55 WHIP, the highest of any starter on today’s board. He has thrown 40.2 innings with two starts, mostly out of the bullpen, at 11.07 strikeouts per nine. A pitcher who strikes out that many and walks that many is capable of anything in either direction. No price was posted at publication.

The Best Record In Baseball

Rangers at Brewers

Sunday, 2:10 PM ET | American Family Field, Milwaukee, WI

Milwaukee is 85-51, the best record on this board and in the sport, and the case is not subtle. The Brewers have allowed 499 runs in 137 games with a 3.36 ERA, a 1.16 WHIP, a .219 opponent average and 1,313 strikeouts, the highest total of any club playing today. Texas is 66-70 and has scored 547 runs, the second fewest here.

Dustin May starts for Milwaukee at 6-8 with a 4.52 ERA, a 1.30 WHIP and a .255 opponent average over 125.1 innings. He is comfortably the weakest link in that rotation and the club has won anyway, which is what a bullpen at 3.36 buys you.

Kumar Rocker answers for Texas at 5-10 with a 4.41 ERA and a 1.41 WHIP across 126.2 innings and 24 starts. The two starting lines are close enough to be a coin flip. Everything after the fifth inning is not. No number was posted at publication.

Ashcraft At 13-5

Pirates at Cardinals

Sunday, 2:15 PM ET | Busch Stadium, St. Louis, MO

Two clubs a game either side of .500 with nothing left to play for, and the best pitcher in the game is the one nobody outside Pennsylvania has heard about. Pittsburgh is 66-71 and has struck out 1,264 batters, third most on this board. St. Louis is 68-69.

Braxton Ashcraft is 13-5 with a 3.59 ERA, a 1.07 WHIP and a .230 opponent average across 155.1 innings and 26 starts, with 9.33 strikeouts per nine. That is a full workload season at rates most rotations would build around, on a club that finished nowhere.

Matthew Liberatore counters at 5-12 with a 5.40 ERA, a 1.46 WHIP and a .283 opponent average over 130 innings, the highest ERA of any starter today with more than sixty innings. The gap between the two starting lines is the widest on the board outside Rogers Centre. No price was posted at publication.

Eight Innings Of Experience

Astros at Mets

Sunday, 3:10 PM ET | Citi Field, Queens, NY

For the second straight day Houston is sending something close to a bullpen game into Citi Field. The Astros are 68-68 and have allowed 664 runs with a 4.64 ERA. The Mets are 61-75 and have scored 559 runs with a .694 OPS, the second lowest offensive figure on the board.

Ethan Pecko starts for Houston with eight major league innings to his name, a 7.88 ERA and a 1.88 WHIP across two starts. That is the smallest sample of anyone taking the ball today apart from Mason Adams in Atlanta, and the numbers inside it are not encouraging.

Zac Thornton answers at 3-4 with a 2.88 ERA and a 1.15 WHIP over 56.1 innings and ten starts. He does not miss many bats at 6.39 per nine, so the run prevention is coming from soft contact rather than strikeouts. New York was posted at -122 with the total at 9.

A 5.41 Staff ERA In Sacramento

Orioles at Athletics

Sunday, 4:05 PM ET | Sutter Health Park, Sacramento, CA

The highest total on the board and the reason is on both sides of it. The Athletics are 53-83 and have allowed 779 runs in 136 games with a 5.41 ERA, a 1.50 WHIP and a .269 opponent average. Baltimore is 67-69 and has allowed 639 with a 4.19.

Chris Bassitt starts for Baltimore at 5-4 with a 4.74 ERA, a 1.55 WHIP and a .301 opponent average across 74 innings. That opponent average is the highest of any starter working today and by a wide margin.

Jeffrey Springs answers at 3-12 with a 6.08 ERA and a 1.50 WHIP over 118.1 innings in 24 starts. Two starters with a WHIP at or above 1.50 in a minor league ballpark in late August is how a total gets to 10. Baltimore was posted at -152 with the number at 10.

Wheeler At 10.71 Per Nine

Phillies at Angels

Sunday, 4:07 PM ET | Angel Stadium, Anaheim, CA

The biggest mismatch on the board on paper. Philadelphia is 76-60 with 1,340 strikeouts, the most of any club playing today. The Angels are 52-84 and have scored 553 runs with a .681 OPS, tied with Seattle for the least productive offense on this slate.

Zack Wheeler is 10-5 with a 3.40 ERA, a 1.09 WHIP, a .213 opponent average and 10.71 strikeouts per nine across 124.1 innings, the second highest strikeout rate of any starter taking the ball today.

Yusei Kikuchi counters at 0-4 with a 6.25 ERA, a 1.58 WHIP and a .294 opponent average over 36 innings. That WHIP is the highest figure on the entire board. Philadelphia was posted at -177 with the total at 7.5, which is the lowest number of the day and a statement about the two lineups rather than the two starters.

Burns At 14-3

Reds at Cubs

Sunday, 7:20 PM ET | Wrigley Field, Chicago, IL

The national television game and the best starting pitcher on the board is wearing the visiting uniform. Chicago is 77-59 and has scored 720 runs in 136 games, the most of any club playing today by a clear margin. Cincinnati is 64-72 and hits .228, the second lowest average on the board.

Chase Burns is 14-3 with a 2.77 ERA, a 1.15 WHIP, a .218 opponent average and 10.57 strikeouts per nine across 139.2 innings and 25 starts. Only Rasmussen has a lower ERA today and nobody with a full season workload combines that ERA with that strikeout rate.

Shota Imanaga answers at 8-9 with a 3.78 ERA, a 1.12 WHIP and a .235 opponent average over 145.1 innings. The Cubs staff sits at 4.12 as a group, which is the honest counterweight: Imanaga is considerably better than the bullpen behind him. Chicago was posted at -157 with the total at 9.
